Taken from [Recipe Tin Eats easy chocolate brownie recipe](https://www.recipetineats.com/easy-chocolate-brownies/)

## Ingredients

- 200g unsalted butter
- 200 g dark chocolate chips 
- 1 cup (175g) brown sugar
- 3 eggs , lightly beaten
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 75g plain flour
- 30g cocoa powder
- Pinch of salt
- 1.5 cups roughly chopped walnuts

## Method

- Preheat oven to 180 degrees celsius
- Combine butter and choc chips in bowl and microwave until melted (30 sec bursts until 1m30s)
- Add melted combination to sugar and vanilla - mix - add eggs and mix until smooth and molten
- Add flour, cocoa and salt - stir until smooth - add walnuts and stir more
- Spray a baking tin, line with baking paper, and pour in mixture
- Bake for 28/29 minutes
- Rest for 10 minutes before removing baking paper (and brownies) from baking tin
- Cool for at least 20 minutes more
- Cut and serve
