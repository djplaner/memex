A mishmash of elements developed over time drawing from different sources all in an attempt to recapture my first experience of Buffalo Wings in a Marriot hotel in the 90s (LA I think)

Follow [recipe tin eats' buffalo wings recipe](https://www.recipetineats.com/truly-crispy-oven-baked-buffalo-wings-my-wings-cookbook/#recipe) for preparing the chicken, but use the buffalo and blue cheese sauce below.

## Ingredients

Blue cheese sauce

- 2/3 cup of sour cream
- 1/2 cup of mayonnaise
- clove of garlic
- 2 teaspoons or Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup of crumbled blue cheese
- salt
- ground pepper
- 2 tablespoons (more or less) milk
- celery (and carrot) sticks

Buffalo sauce

- 3 to 5 tablespoons Hot pepper sauce
- 4 tablespoons low fat butter
- 1 tablespoon of white wine vinegar

## Method

Blue cheese sauce

- Mix all ingredients save the milk, pepper and salt
- Season to taste
- Add milk to bring to pancake batter consistency
