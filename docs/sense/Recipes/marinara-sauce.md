## Ingredients

- 4 x 400 gram crushed tomatoes
- 170 grams tomato paste
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 large yellow onion diced
- 2 tbsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp dried basil 
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried red chilli flakes
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper 
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar 
- 1 tbsp brown sugar

## Method

- Combine all ingredients in slow cooker
- Set for 8 hours on low
- Remove bay leaves
- Optional: use blender to puree to preferred consistency
