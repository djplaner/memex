Started during 2024 our focus on bush regeneration (aka [[ecosystem-restoration]]) is on our 10 acre block - [[wood-duck-meadows]]. This page offers an index to related resources and work.

## Our approach

Still in the early days, but our approach will not be straight [[ecosystem-restoration]]. We have pondered, but not yet implemented insights from [[permaculture]] and [[permaculture-principles]]. At least not beyond the idea of obtaining a yield. For example, the planning of fruit trees that aren't native (e.g. [[mango-nam-doc-mai]], [[loquat-lower-dam]]).

An early part of the work was volunteering with the [Friends of Escarpment Parks](https://fep.org.au/) at [Redwood Park](https://fep.org.au/our-parks/redwood-park/). Redwood Park is close in nature to our context (our northern boundary is a creek which passes through Redwood Park).
