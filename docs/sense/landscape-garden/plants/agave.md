[Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agave) of succulents. Listed by the Queensland Government as an [invasive species](https://www.publications.qld.gov.au/ckan-publications-attachments-prod/resources/fc54295d-5cb5-424f-a44e-a375ce678087/agave.pdf?ETag=ccb91dd74e222b4148811983b092380d)

A previous owner appears to have planted a fair bit of Agave on [[wood-duck-meadows]]. In particular, the [[gatton-creek-frontage]] had a lot of agave planted along the creek bank. While potentially serving positively to hold the bank together and limit the spread of the multitude of invasive plants from the creek, it was also spreading further into the property. The following image shows some of agave as part of clearing up the [[gatton-creek-frontage]].
