The Queensland government defines various [regional ecosystems](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/plants-animals/plants/ecosystems/about) as "vegetation communities in a bioregion that are consistently associated with a particular combination of geology, landform and soil". Our block ([[wood-duck-meadows]]) includes two main regional ecosystems. 

## [12.3.7 Eucalyptus tereticornis (Blue Gum) fringing forests](https://apps.des.qld.gov.au/regional-ecosystems/details/?re=12.3.7), and

This are the areas of the property actually in and closely surrounding the Gatton and Rocky Creek's that form our northern boundary. In particular, parts of the [[gatton-creek-frontage]] and [[rocky-creek-frontage]]

- Local council on [planting lists and revegetation 12.3.7](https://www.lockyervalley.qld.gov.au/repository/libraries/id:2eccbxg5l17q9su8pzhy/hierarchy/our-services/strategic-planning/documents/planning-studies/Revegetation%20Species%20List%20for%20Regional%20Ecosystem%2012.3.7.pdf)
- Healthy land and water's [factsheet for RE 12.3.7](https://www.lfwseq.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/RE-Fact-Sheet_12.3.7_HLW.pdf)

## [12.3.3 Blue Gum woodlands on alluvial flats](https://apps.des.qld.gov.au/regional-ecosystems/details/?re=12.3.3). 

Due to widespread clearing this regional ecosystem is classed as _endangered_. Much of this RE is cleared on [[wood-duck-meadows]]. There remain evidence of Queensland Blue Gums, mostly nearer or part of the [[gatton-creek-frontage]] and [[rocky-creek-frontage]]

- Local council on [planting lists and revegetation for 12.3.3](https://www.lockyervalley.qld.gov.au/repository/libraries/id:2eccbxg5l17q9su8pzhy/hierarchy/our-services/strategic-planning/documents/planning-studies/Revegetation%20Species%20List%20for%20Regional%20Ecosystem%2012.3.3.pdf) 
- Healthy land and water's [factsheet for RE 12.3.3](https://www.lfwseq.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/RE-Fact-Sheet_12.3.3_HLW.pdf)
