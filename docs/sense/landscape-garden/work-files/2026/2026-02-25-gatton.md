First step was tidying up the main bank. Removing various nasties (e.g. small [[schinus-terebinthifolia]], [[asparagus-africanus]], [[solanum-seaforthianum]] etc) in an on-going battle.

Finally got back down to the big rock in Gatton Creek just below the roundabout. Like much of both Gatton and Rocky Creeks its overgrown by a large collection of different nasties ([[celtis-sinensis|Chinese hackberry]], [[salvia-coccinea]] etc). But the original focus was on finishing off the [[schinus-terebinthifolia]] invasive trees just below the rock.  

After all that work, the rock's visibility is much improved. Though not readily apparent from the following photo. At the very least the She Oak that has been growing horizontally across the rock is visible from above.
