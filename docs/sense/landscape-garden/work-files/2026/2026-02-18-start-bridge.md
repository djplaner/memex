Commenced early planning and work on the [[island-bridge]]. The first step was to commence the long task of sourcing the building material by moving rocks used in old garden bed edging. Starting with the gardens around the  [[roundabout]].

<figure markdown>
![The first source of material for the Island bridge](https://djon.es/assets/memex/sense/landscape-garden/work-history/2026/rock-source.jpeg)
<caption>The first source of material for the Island bridge</caption>
</figure>

The rocks were moved to the planned site for the bridge on the southern bank of [[the-dam]]. As well as dropping the rocks some weeding work was undertaken, early steps in preparing the site.

![The planned site for the Island bridge](https://djon.es/assets/memex/sense/landscape-garden/work-history/2026/bridge-site.jpeg)
