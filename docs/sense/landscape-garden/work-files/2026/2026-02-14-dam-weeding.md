There were also a few additional "findings" whilst doing this work, including:

- Discovering a couple of very small [[338503174|Black tea tree]] saplings.
- (re-)finding a small [[338491730]] Lamboto tree.
- Identifying a [[338491048|confusing spur-throated locust]].
