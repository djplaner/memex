After moving down the creek a fair way and after a couple of weeks laid up with foot problems it was time to get back to the start of the [[riparian-restoration]] work. Cleaning out a section below the bank and into the creek bed. Mostly overgrown with [[neonotonia-wightii]] (Glycine) with some evidence of [[schinus-terebinthifolia]] and [[anredera-cordifolia]] (Maderia)


<figure markdown>
![Largely overgrown with glycine, ready for removal](https://djon.es/assets/memex/sense/landscape-garden/work-history/2026/fig_gatton_before.jpeg)
<caption>Largely overgrown with glycine, ready for removal</caption>
</figure>

After removal there were a few surprises. Rather than a slope into the water there were quite a few significant lumps of soil apparently created through soil being gathered by roots of existing trees.
