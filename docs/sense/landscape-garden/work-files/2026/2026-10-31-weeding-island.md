After spending a bit of the morning weeding around the [[roundabout]] and [[gatton-creek-frontage]] it was time for the island. Heavy recent rain had pressed the go button on a lot of nascent weeds, especially the [[anredera-cordifolia|Madeira vine]]. Also readily apparent that it was helping a lot of the natives. The island's [[pleiogynium-timoriense|burdekin plum]] had new leaves dotting its recently bare branches. Similarly the [[ficus-opposita|sandpaper fig]] which also had a good sprinkling of fruit developing. Most other trees were looking much improved and the tea trees were starting to flower.

On the plus side, there did appear to be a couple of short-flowered flax lilly spontaneously growing on the island. Hopeful early signs of unassisted restoration.
