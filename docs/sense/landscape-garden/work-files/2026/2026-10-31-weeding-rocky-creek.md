A fairly dry Saturday - after a long period of rain - has me out starting some very much needed weeding. The first of many, many such tasks over the coming months. In the morning, I started on the [[roundabout]] and [[rocky-creek-frontage]]. Initially cleaning up regrowth on [[schinus-terebinthifolia|pepper]] and [[schotia-brachypetala|drunken parrot]] trees that had been previously cut back. However, also picking up or simply noting lots of other returning favourites, including Cats Claw, Madeira vine, [[leucaena-leucocephala]], various nightshades etc. In particular, a fair bit coming back down on the creek.

On the plus side, it was good to see how clear the creeks are now. Apparently after the good flush out they received from the mini-floods from all the rain.
