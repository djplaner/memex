The bunker's parasitic ficus is still hanging on. It's gone through a phase of good growth, including hosting 3 or 4 paper wasp nests. However, currently it appears to be in a bit of a shutdown. All leaves shown in the above image are gone, but there is evidence of life with new growth appearing at the branch tips.

On the aesthetic front, I found this image quite striking once seen on a bigger screen. The weathered whites and greys of the concrete causing the ficus to almost disappear.
