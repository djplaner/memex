Come February 2026 and it's time to focus on the riparian sections of the place on the fringes of the [[roundabout]], [[gatton-creek-frontage]], and [[rocky-creek-frontage]] zones. Zones that had become overgrown with an array of invasive species and ripe for some [[riparian-restoration]].

First up was the edge of the [[roundabout]], just below the big fig. Some [[schinus-terebinthifolia]], [[celtis-sinensis]], [[ricinus-communis]] trees on the larger end and the start of the glycine infestation.

The starting end edged into the [[gatton-creek-frontage]] and involved climbing down one of the large boulders to cut down some [[schinus-terebinthifolia]]
