Visible from the roundabout is the fork of Rocky and Gatton creeks (we've never been certain what the creek is called afterward, online sources vary). It's been the focus of a fair bit of clearing in the last couple of months. Late October brought close to 100mm of rain (unexpected with a looming super El Nino) and has both creeks flooding.
