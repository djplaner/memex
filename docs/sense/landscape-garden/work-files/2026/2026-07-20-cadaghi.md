[[corymbia-torelliana]] (Cadaghi) trees are an on-going question: to remove, or not to remove. Latest advice to July 2026 was to remove only to keep numbers low, especially young species. The [[rocky-creek-frontage]] (near the [[lower-dam]]) had one established tree that appeared quite successful at procreation. Both within our fence line and close by. Time to remove the younger examples.
