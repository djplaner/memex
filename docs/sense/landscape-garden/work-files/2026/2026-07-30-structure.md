Our first explicit step in trying to transform the [[horse-paddock]] through adding some 'structure' to the wide open grassland (essentially a mown lawn). Following advice briefly alluded to by [this technical note](https://www.lfwseq.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/LWF-Tech-Notes-GENERAL-G2_Wildlife-Corridors-WEB.pdf) the following documents the start of a pile of [[corymbia-torelliana]] (Cadaghi) tree trunks. 

Penrith City Council - of all sources - [explains the rationale for piles of woody waste in bush regeneration](https://www.penrithcity.nsw.gov.au/waste-environment/environment/bushland/why-are-there-woody-weed-piles-in-bushland), including this on [fauna refuge log piles](https://www.aabr.org.au/fauna-refuge-log-piles/).
