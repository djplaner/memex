In 2026 we decided to start the design and construction of a stone "bridge" to [[the-island]]. Arguably more a weir than a bridge. It will hopefully enable access to [[the-island]] during the wettest of seasons.

## Planning

The initial vague plan is to re-purpose the large collection of rocks that have been used to surround garden beds. Stack those rocks with (clay) soil to form a solid weir/bridge with a walking path on top.

Initial design features, include:

- Foundation dug out of the channel. Spray the foundation with herbicide and line with pebbles/crushed concrete.
- Foundation will be wider than the top providing a sloped edge.
- Pack the rocks with the local clay soil dug out of the foundation.
- Perhaps top with soil to provide the walking path.
