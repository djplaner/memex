Forewarned that applying for an India e-visa is an "experience" and stumbling on the pre-login, I decided to track the experience.

Getting over the wrinkles (and there are many) and it all worked and visas successful.

## Log

- Pre-login creation

    First page asks for info about nationality, type of passport, email address (and other initial stuff). However, it appears to have a timeout set that opens a modal requesting passport number and other details even if you haven't completed the other information. A modal that cannot be closed until information is provided. After providing this information you are asked if you've visited the DRC in the last 21 days. On completion you are returned to the pre-login page. After selecting nationality (the first required information) another modal pops up informing you of what you've chosen. An event that only happens for this information on this page.

    Towards the end you're asked to designate the purpose of travel from a drop down that provides a long list of options all written in line with the various flavours of e-visa. Entering the date for arrival doesn't allow free-text entry of the date, unlike the query for birth date. Apparently because possible dates are selected, requiring use of the calendar widget.

    On clicking "continue" a modal informing of the requirement to upload scan of passport details page is provided...twice.

- Application details page

    - Religion drop down does not provide a na, though the national id textbox does.
    - Not sure I have any _visible identification marks_
    - The sizing of the form means some bad wrapping in places

- Applicant details page 

    - Both phone and mobile are compulsory, even if you don't have one.

- Details of visa sought

    - 2 short textboxes to outline where you will visit.
    - Countries visited in last 10 years - 1 short textbox
    - Reference - provide the name and details of someone you know in India. A pause here whilst we figure out the requirements for htis.

- Restarting

    Due to a pause whilst exploring referencing, there's a need to restart....and the application has forgotten some of where I was up to. 
    
    - Manual refilling required. The captcha used on restarting also shows its age.
    - Oh, and now it's stalled, no it is back. A good 30+ second wait there.
    - Yep, it's forgotten a lot of information. Appears that if you don't complete a page, it forgets everything that you had completed. The "reference" was last on a quite detailed page. All gone.
    - This details page also appears to include some Javascript. With the slow connection it is taking a long time to load. Trying to enter information before it finishes results in a reload.

- Timeout

    Now it's timed out and I have to start again, reentering all the same forgotten information.

- Upload photo

    Oh the pain. Once uploaded the photo had to be cropped. The interactive cropping tool only allows for a square cropping area. If your image is rectangular this can make it impossible to find a square area of the photo that includes your entire face. My solution was to copy the photo into another image with a larger horizontal dimension.

- Upload scanned passport information

    Apparently this had to be in PDF format. I'd assumed an image would be acceptable. Had to convert the image to PDF and then reduce the size of the PDF. The latter a new thing to learn.

Success. A day later and an email reports success.

And now onto applying for my spouse

- Pre-login creation 

    Wouldn't work. Couldn't get off this page, wouldn't accepted "Continue". Changed to a different browser and all worked. Subsequently had to stop that application and was able to restart on the original browser. Only this pre-login page wasn't working.

- Student requires employment from spouse or father

    Apparently the idea of a mature student with a retired husband wasn't one of the scenarios gamed by the designers of this site.
