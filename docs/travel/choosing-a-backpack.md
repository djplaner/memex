With a fair bit of travelling to come I'm in the market for a travel backpack.


!!! success "Purchased an AER Travel Pack 4"

    Distillation of the reviews and looking at features suggested it the best fit for me. Icing on the cake was the 25% off Xmas deal from Rushfaster.

## Baggage allowances

- Air New Zealand 36x21x50cm (w-d-h)

    But also says "maximum linear dimensions (L+W+H) of 118cm (46.5in)"

- Singapore - length/width/height not to exceed 115cm
